🔒 Privacy Policy – Kouba Application

Last Updated: August 26, 2026 | Effective Date: Immediately upon public deployment of the application

Kouba respects your privacy and is committed to complying with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, and Law No. 53-05 on the electronic exchange of legal data.

📌 Data Controller: Kouba Laayoune (Sole Proprietorship), registered address: Laayoune, Kingdom of Morocco. Email: admin@kouba.site.

1. Information We Collect

a. Basic Account Information (All Users)

  • Full name, email address, mobile phone number, city/address, profile picture (optional).

b. Driver-Specific Data

  • National ID number, professional driving license, vehicle/tanker photos, insurance, health and technical inspection certificates, default water source (Ayafa/Tantan), current tank capacity (updated every 12 hours).

c. Location and Usage

  • Geolocation data (GPS) (only when the app is active for clients; for drivers during active trips), device information, app version, order details, ratings, transaction history.

d. Payment Information

  • Digital wallet balance, transaction history, voucher codes. We do not store credit card numbers – payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (Law 09-08 and GDPR)

  • Contract performance: To provide delivery services, process payments, and manage your account.
  • Legitimate interests: To improve the application, prevent fraud, and ensure security.
  • Consent: For marketing communications and personalised advertising (you may withdraw consent at any time).
  • Legal obligation: To comply with Moroccan laws and respond to judicial orders.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing core services (matching, tracking, payments).
  • Improving the application, fixing bugs, analysing usage.
  • Sending order notifications, updates, support responses, and marketing communications (with your consent).
  • Verifying driver identity, preventing fraud.
  • Complying with legal obligations (CNDP, judicial orders).
  • Generating aggregated (non-personal) statistics.
  • Operating the rating and reputation system.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your information. Limited sharing occurs only for:

  • Between client and driver – necessary to complete the trip.
  • Technical service providers:
    • Dedicated servers (hosted in Europe) – secure hosting with full encryption.
    • Push notification services – for order notifications and updates.
    • Payment gateways – for processing digital payments (we do not store sensitive payment details).
  • Legal/judicial authorities – when Moroccan law requires it.
  • With your explicit consent – e.g., for marketing or research.

5. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers outside Morocco (secure European data centres). These transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) ensuring an adequate level of data protection in accordance with Moroccan law and international standards.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember preferences, analyse usage, and improve security. For full details, see our Cookies Policy.

7. Advertising Consent and Marketing

When you first register, we will ask for your separate consent to:

  • Send promotional notifications (special offers, new features).
  • Use anonymised data to display relevant ads within the application.
  • Share non-sensitive usage data with advertising partners (only if you agree).

You may change your mind at any time through the app settings or by contacting us. See our Advertising Consent page for more details.

8. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on secure dedicated servers in European data centres (GDPR-compliant).
  • Encryption: TLS 1.3 for transmission, encryption at rest.
  • Retention: Active account data is retained; upon deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days.
  • In the event of a data breach, we notify affected users and CNDP within 72 hours.

9. Automated Decision-Making

Our matching algorithm uses geographic filtering, tank capacity, and water source to suggest drivers. This is not fully automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning you. You may always contact support for human intervention.

10. Your Rights (Law 09-08)

  • Access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), objection to direct marketing, data portability, restriction of processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You may file a complaint with the Moroccan Data Protection Authority (CNDP) at www.cndp.ma or by post to Rabat.
  • To exercise your rights, send an email to admin@kouba.site. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

Kouba is not directed at users under 18 without parental consent. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a minor without consent, we will delete it immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. You will be notified of material changes via email or in-app notification.

13. Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by Moroccan law. Disputes are first resolved amicably; otherwise, the competent courts of Laayoune have jurisdiction.
