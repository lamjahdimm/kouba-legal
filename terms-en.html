6. Pricing and Payments

  • The base service price is variable by city: reflecting local currency differences and the specific operational costs determined by Kouba.
  • Kouba does not charge for the water itself. We exclusively charge facilitation fees for connecting and servicing between clients and drivers. The actual price of the water delivered is a matter of commodity pricing and may be regulated by Kouba's internal policies or by government regulations. Kouba's service does not interfere with or set the price of the water commodity itself.
  • All prices are in Moroccan dirhams and include taxes (if applicable).
  • Facilitation fees are non-refundable once the delivery is successfully completed.
  • All financial transactions are recorded and verifiable through the public cryptographic proof system.
  • We reserve the right to modify facilitation fees or the pricing structure. Any change will be announced via email or in-app notification at least 30 days before it takes effect.

7. Cancellations and Fee Transfers

  • Client cancellation: Free within 30 minutes of the driver's acceptance.
  • Cancellation after matching (by driver or client): If an order is cancelled after a match has been made, a cancellation fee of 60% of the base service price is transferred to the "waiting party" (the party who did not cancel and was waiting for the service).
  • Example: If a driver cancels after a match where the base price is 5 MAD, 60% (i.e., 3 MAD) is moved from the driver's applicable balance to the client's wallet, together with the recorded cancellation reason. This transaction is permanently recorded in:
    • • The client's wallet history,
    • • The driver's wallet history, and
    • • The delivery history of both parties.
  • Automatic cancellation: If no driver accepts within 5 minutes, the order is automatically cancelled without any fees.
  • All cancellation fee transfers are fully verifiable through the public cryptographic proof system.

8. Data Integrity and Cryptographic Proof

Kouba is committed to the highest standards of transparency and data integrity. We have implemented a public cryptographic proof system, but Kouba does not perform verification on your behalf. For the system to be trustworthy, you—or an independent third party—must be able to verify your data without relying on Kouba's servers. For full details, please read the Cryptographic Proof Policy.

  • Public publication: Every seven days, Kouba publishes its cryptographic commitments (a compact cryptographic root) on a public ledger. This data remains publicly accessible for one month, then is archived and retained for two additional years.
  • Independent verification: Users or any third party can verify their proof path against the public root. This verification must be performed outside of Kouba's infrastructure.
  • Partially public source code: The code for the wallet system, payment processing, and admin panel is exposed to the public to allow independent auditing, while other components (matching, routing, location services) remain proprietary.
  • Administrative access restrictions: There is no direct access to the database. Any administrative access requires:
    • • A support ticket stating the purpose, scope, and duration,
    • • A written statement explaining the necessity of access,
    • • Signed approval from the Data Protection Officer (DPO) or an authorised representative, and
    • • An immutable audit trail for each access operation.
